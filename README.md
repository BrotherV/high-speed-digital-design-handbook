## About the book

*A Practical Handbook for Fast Circuit Boards* is a technical reference for hardware and PCB design engineers and graduate students working with high-speed digital systems. It brings together explanations, engineering approximations, worked examples, design rules, and checklists for designing and bringing up multilayer circuit boards.

The book has 43 chapters in eight parts, nine appendices, 238 figures, and more than 100 worked examples. Each worked example shows its arithmetic, checks that the result is valid, and ends with the design consequence. Each chapter ends with review problems, and the answers are collected at the back of the book. Values taken from standards are cited, and every figure states what kind of evidence it is: analytical, simulation, schematic, or illustrative.

## Topics covered

- **Part I — Foundations:**
  - The PCB as an electrical system.
  - What makes a design high-speed.
  - Electromagnetics and transmission-line theory.
  - A working vocabulary of signals, symbols, and skew.
- **Part II — Signal Integrity:**
  - Reflections, termination networks, stack-up and impedance control.
  - Impedance sensitivity and fabrication tolerance.
  - Losses, crosstalk, differential signaling, S-parameters.
  - Vias, routing geometry and length matching, timing, jitter, and eye diagrams.
- **Part III — Power Integrity:**
  - Transient current and the PDN response.
  - How inductance and decoupling capacitors shape rail impedance and ripple.
  - Ground bounce, target-impedance PDN design, capacitor technologies, planes, and return paths.
- **Part IV — Power Conversion, Sequencing, and Thermal Design:** power-rail architecture and regulator design, power sequencing and reset, and thermal design of the board.
- **Part V — EMI and EMC:** radiated emission mechanisms, FCC and CISPR limits, shielding and apertures, and board-level layout for low emissions.
- **Part VI — Interfaces in Practice:**
  - A PCB layout and routing chapter: stack-ups, return paths, BGA breakout, delay matching, differential-pair routing practice, and layout walkthroughs.
  - DDR4/DDR5, LPDDR4/LPDDR5, PCI Express, USB/Type-C, Ethernet, MIPI D-PHY/C-PHY, and clock distribution.
  - Connectors, plus SATA, HDMI, DisplayPort, LVDS, CAN, and RS-485.
  - Low-speed serial buses: SPI, I²C, SMBus, I3C, and UART.
- **Part VII — Simulation, Measurement, and Bring-Up:** modeling and simulation, TDR, VNA, oscilloscope and near-field measurements, board bring-up and debugging, and design for manufacturing and release.
- **Part VIII — Case Studies:** seven short case studies, and a full design study of a modular ARM and FPGA embedded platform (an RK3588 SoM and a Zynq-7020 SoM on a carrier board).

The appendices include:
- reference formulas, material properties, design-rule checklists, and unit conversions with rules of thumb;
- Python calculations;
- interface quick-reference sheets and a glossary;
- study paths with integrated exercises;
- review templates and troubleshooting trees.

## Intended readers

Practicing hardware engineers, PCB designers, and graduate students with a background in circuit theory and some exposure to electromagnetics. The handbook develops the necessary transmission-line concepts from first principles.
