## About the book

*A Practical Handbook for Fast Circuit Boards* is a technical reference for hardware and PCB design engineers and graduate students working with high-speed digital systems. It brings together explanations, engineering approximations, worked examples, design rules, and checklists for designing and bringing up multilayer circuit boards.

## Topics covered

- **Part I — Foundations:** PCB anatomy, fast edges, electromagnetics, and transmission-line theory.
- **Part II — Signal Integrity:** reflections, termination, controlled impedance, losses, crosstalk, differential pairs, vias, jitter, and eye diagrams.
- **Part III — Power Integrity:** voltage droop, target impedance, power distribution networks, ground bounce, and return paths.
- **Part IV — EMI and EMC:** emissions mechanisms and design practices.
- **Part V — Interfaces in Practice:** DDR4/DDR5, LPDDR4/LPDDR5, PCI Express, USB/Type-C, Ethernet, MIPI, and clock distribution.
- **Part VI — Simulation, Measurement, and Bring-Up:** modeling, TDR, VNA, oscilloscope measurements, debugging, and manufacturing release.

The appendices include reference formulas, design-rule checklists, material properties, Python calculations, a 10-layer SoC board case study, interface quick-reference sheets, a glossary, and integrated exercises.

## Intended readers

Practicing hardware engineers, PCB designers, and graduate students with a background in circuit theory and some exposure to electromagnetics. The handbook develops the necessary transmission-line concepts from first principles.
